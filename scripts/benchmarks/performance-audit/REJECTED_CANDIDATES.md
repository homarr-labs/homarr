## Rejected widget prototypes

The Coolify memo and cluster rendering candidates were removed after these separate candidate measurements. Every selected scenario is shown, including small/large improvements and regressions. Their final-source rows below use the restored original implementation; identical-source timing differences are noise. The candidate fixture, production source hashes, raw pairs and rejection reasons remain archived. Timings cover local React fixture operations and do not establish dashboard performance. Paired wins compare each same-trial before/after pair; changes compare separate side medians, so these statistics can differ.

- **WIDGETS_B_006:** Reverted both added memos under the measured-value and code-deletion gate. Builds fall from 9 to 1 on first 9-render sequence and 9 to 0 when warmed, but typical single CPU median rises 1.5%; card 20 wall median rises 11.6% and CPU 1.6%; card 1 CPU rises 10.1%; card 1000 CPU rises 0.6%. Single 1/single 1000 wall and CPU medians improve, and card 1000 wall improves, but paired results are variable and common card paths regress. Deterministic work/allocation savings do not establish a consistent CPU benefit sufficient for extra hooks and retained Maps. Typical 3-node wall/CPU medians improve at submicrosecond scale; tiny wall improves but independent CPU median rises 44.6% despite 7 of 9 paired CPU wins. Mixed numeric outcomes do not justify replacing clear existing reductions.
