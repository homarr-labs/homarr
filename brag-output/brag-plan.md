# Brag plan: Homarr 2.0

## Planning rubric
1. **App:** A self-hosted dashboard for apps, service data and controls.
2. **Strongest claim:** Custom Widgets and Workshop let the community build and share new dashboard ideas without waiting for a Homarr release.
3. **Visual hook:** A populated Homarr board emerges from its service icons: “Your server. Your starting point.”
4. **Actual UI:** The release dashboard, Calendar drag recording, onboarding studio, Assistant, workbench, Workshop and header studio.
5. **Shortest satisfying full film:** 80 seconds. The requested full-release coverage and two catalog marquees justify exceeding Brag's 15–25 second teaser default.
6. **Tone:** Polished; an energetic product demonstration for self-hosters, with clear holds and real UI.
7. **Audio:** Steady clean bed, Brag's Business Moves vol. 12, restrained clicks at meaningful interactions. No voice.
8. **Caption:** Your apps, your services, your dashboard. Homarr 2.0 brings rebuilt board editing, Assistant, Custom Widgets and Workshop to your self-hosted setup.
9. **User flow:** Connect services → arrange a board → inspect and act with Assistant → build/share a custom widget.

## Identity and composition
1920×1080 landscape, 30fps, 80 seconds. Inter from Homarr's theme; red `#fa5252`, dark `#242424`, text `#f8f9fa`. Actual Homarr SVG logo. A consistent dark stage with small red structural accents frames product images without recoloring them. Big short headlines; product screens occupy most of the frame. Simulated interactions are identified as illustrative in the source notes.
