# Configurable Beszel thresholds

On 2026-10-19, the maintainer rejected configurable metric color thresholds for the Beszel Grid, Table, and Stats widgets, including Off, Standard, and Custom modes.

Keep the existing hardcoded color behavior. The maintainer considered the proposed configuration over-engineered and chose to retain the existing colors rather than add warning and critical threshold controls.
