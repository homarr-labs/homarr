## Local activation behavior

`useWidgetInputActivation` commits current admission in layout, independently of preview ownership. It compares actual board/client/query identities and the relevant semantic fields: editor item/kind/raw definition/initial baseline/section, or picker destination section/column count. Upstream captured currentness supplies the authority/presentation epoch; callbacks and wrapper objects are not compared as epochs.

For a committed admitted activation, the join captures upstream once, creates a new lease and requests the publication render. Its provider owner is that fixed lease, not a function which obtains a new lease during mutation. Until publication, callers receive a denied owner. Retained callbacks close over their published owner; they never read a replacement owner as their origin.

Currentness requires the local lease to remain active and equal the current lease, the semantic binding to match committed live binding, the captured upstream owner to remain current, and the committed current-owner/admission predicates to permit the operation. Immediate submission/integration/closing refs participate in admission. A failed check irreversibly retires that lease. Loss, replacement and unmount retire it; later admission allocates a different lease, never setting an old lease active again.

Ordinary form edits, validation updates, specialized dirty revisions and preview generation changes do not rotate input activation. The real form object is not copied/decorated and no second form/draft, baseline reset or rollback is introduced.

Because useForm retains its initial seed, a mounted WidgetEditor is not admitted under a different board/item/kind/definition/initial-baseline/section identity. U must remount for that different initial editor lifetime; W does not silently re-seed the genuine form. Client/query or authority/presentation replacement can publish a new activation when the original form identity still matches and the upstream producer genuinely admits it.

Retirement occurs before `submitting.current = true`, `integrationCreationLock.current = true`, opening discard, actual close, `onStaged` and the corresponding host-changing continuations. Discard cancellation and completed local pending phases can publish a fresh admitted capture through a new render. Closing remains denied while the same opened presentation is closing. Existing captured preview patches keep their independent generation checks and merge into the actual current form options unchanged.

AddWidget's picker capture is committed before its callbacks are supplied. Selection starts from that previously captured real initiator, retains the actual destination, and checks that origin after definition/refetch/integration awaits. The selected editor receives that fixed selection origin; its capture callback returns the captured origin rather than acquiring today's upstream owner. Picker retirement does not renew a stale selection or migrate it to another destination. Invalid selection origins retire permanently and are removed on committed loss; the original real form seed is created only for an admitted new selection.
