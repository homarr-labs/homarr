- Scope cleanup now fences new scope admission, snapshots already available responses, and **initiates unused-body cancellation before its owned scope abort and dispatcher destruction**. - Every returned response records its actual fetch signal, dispatcher identity and owned/borrowed provenance. Responses arriving after the initial snapshot are processed only after actual fetch settlement. A rejected late-body cancellation is treated as the expected owned cancellation only when the body was unlocked, its dispatcher is scope-owned and its destruction fulfilled, both the scope and actual fetch signal carry the exact freshly allocated scope-ending reason object, and the cancellation rejection is that same object. The final settled failure batch is retained for the originating quarantine. Existing operation failure/cancellation remains the original rejection when the operation failed; cleanup failure remains independently sticky and is reported by idempotent `closeAsync`. Borrowed dispatchers are still not destroyed by this owner.

Known limitations remain explicit: earlier caller/lifetime/timeout aborts, cloned/wrapped rejection reasons, borrowed-dispatcher disposal and any other late-body failure lacking the exact owned provenance above are not inferred to be successful physical disposal; they conservatively remain quarantined. Pending native settlement is still awaited and can delay drain indefinitely; this correction does not manufacture proof or replace the native transport. The ordering/provenance classification is source-only and awaits the same reviewer's rereview and separately authorized physical proof.
