## 7. Dual-clock handoff, task settlement and publication

### Handoff is decreasing only

Use the retained original mapping for every preparation/dispatch check. Effective deadlines start from the original wall deadline and its mapped monotonic expiry; only tighten for original request/CSRF/validated credential bounds. Preserve all four DB-required timing members unchanged through hashing, body preparation, authority admission, queueing and native dispatch:

`originalWallStartedAt`, `originalMonotonicStartedAt`, `originalBudgetMs`, `originalMonotonicExpiresAt`.

Check positive finite budget, exact original monotonic sum, finite mapped deadlines, non-decreasing observations and approved clock skew. Remaining time is the minimum of original wall budget, original monotonic expiry, effective wall deadline and effective mapped monotonic expiry. Tightening a wall expiry H maps with `originalMonotonicStartedAt + (H - originalWallStartedAt)`, min with all prior deadlines; **never** `performance.now() + (H - Date.now())`. Do not refill budget at preparation, dispatch, scope start or after a clock rollback. DB entry still performs its own capture/validation and native gate checks.

The DB privately obtains actual stored expiry after token/native-policy verification (`statements.ts:145–158,219–228,317–331`). `createOnboardingGate` (`transaction-gate.ts:523–588`) subtracts approved skew, min-clamps validated session hostDeadlineAt and maps to the original clock pair.
