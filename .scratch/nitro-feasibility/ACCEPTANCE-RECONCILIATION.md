The recorded design uses one Nitro/Bun authority/listener, non-streaming manual Router SSR with request-local Router/QueryClient, fixed en/light UI, immutable public/private IDs and owner-only writes, sanitized projections, expected-revision SQLite CAS, absent-only initialization and downstream no-store. Source timeout delay1500ms and raw control-body ownership are bounded in the widget note. These are fixture configuration/source claims, not verified final capacity/close or product guarantees. Database initialization precedes readiness; the final health check uses repository-backed **GET `/api/boards/public`**, superseding packaging's obsolete GET RPC assumption.

Excluded: replacing current Homarr or using real appdata/backups/credentials; production providers/auth/public deployment; full widgets/integrations/editor/Custom JSX/Assistant/media/translations; existing-client compatibility/MCP/OAuth/subscriptions/jobs; PostgreSQL or other engines/full migrations/restores; distributed coordination/replicas/persistent sessions/widget caches; Hono/Elysia/Eden/Start/Nitro2/streaming/GitHub Pages; new automated suites or mandatory resource savings.
