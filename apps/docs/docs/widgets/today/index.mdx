The Today widget presents the current local date without duplicating a live clock. It progressively adds the week
number, day of year, quarter, remaining days, and calendar progress as more space becomes available.

The advanced view shows week, month, and year progress together with a twelve-month overview. Week numbers can follow
your locale or the ISO 8601 convention. “Days after today” excludes the current day to keep the remaining-day count
unambiguous.

The widget runs entirely in your browser and does not send date or location data to an external service. It follows
the timezone and locale of the device viewing the board, so viewers in different regions can see their own local date.
