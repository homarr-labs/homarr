Pick up to four values, such as a Homey or Home Assistant sensor or any numeric [Statistics](/docs/widgets/stats) metric, and follow them over time.

## Where the history comes from

- **Homey and Home Assistant sensors** use the history stored by Homey Insights or the Home Assistant recorder, so their graphs are filled right away.
- **Statistics metrics** are recorded by Homarr while a board with this widget is open. A new graph starts empty and fills up at the refresh interval. Periods without an open board have no recorded values, and the graph connects the values around them.

Recorded history covers at most the last 24 hours at a 30-second interval. It is stored in Redis and is discarded when the integration's URL or credentials change.
