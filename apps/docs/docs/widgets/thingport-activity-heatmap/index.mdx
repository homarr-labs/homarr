This widget shows your Thingport activity for the last 12 months, the same heatmap as on your Thingport profile. Each square is a day, and the busier the day, the darker the square. Hover over a day to see how many models you imported, uploaded, downloaded, opened in a slicer or deleted.

Use the year picker to show an earlier year. When the widget is too narrow for the whole year, it shows the most recent weeks.
