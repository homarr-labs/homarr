### Archived history

By default, the Downloads widget shows SABnzbd's active queue and regular history.

Enable **Include archived SABnzbd history** in the integration settings to also show completed jobs that SABnzbd has moved to its Archive. You can select a history window of **10, 20, or 30 days**.

Archived history is disabled by default. When enabled, the default history window is 10 days. This setting only changes what Homarr displays and does not modify SABnzbd's queue, history, or Archive.
